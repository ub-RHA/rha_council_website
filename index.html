What is RHA?

To represent all students living on campus and build inclusive, engaged communities.

Advocacy

Ensuring the voices of all residents are heard and their safety and well-being prioritized.

Inclusion

Welcoming residents of all backgrounds and identities, with equitable access to campus life.

Community

People who learn and grow from each other and share a sense of care and belonging.

Leadership Development

Experiences that build on residents' strengths to develop them as leaders.
